Sustainably Sourced Beech Logs

At Mendip Beech & Boar, we supply quality beech firewood sourced directly from our own woodland on the eastern edge of the Mendips in Somerset. Our logs come predominantly from wind-blown trees and naturally fallen timber within our mature beech woodland. By making use of timber already on the ground or brought down by the wind, we make the most of our woodland resource while helping to minimise waste.

Every log is cut and processed by hand by us, using our own small-scale equipment. We are a small, independent operation, taking pride in producing quality firewood with a personal touch.

Our beech logs are air-dried naturally in our barn, without energy-intensive artificial drying. Beech is a dense, hard hardwood that produces a lovely, steady heat and burns for longer than many softer woods, making it an excellent choice for cosy evenings around the fire.

We believe in keeping things simple: locally sourced timber, natural air drying and honest firewood from our own woods. With no large-scale production or unnecessary processing, we offer a straightforward, local service with quality at its heart. Choose locally sourced beech firewood and enjoy the warmth of a traditional hardwood fire, knowing exactly where your wood comes from.
